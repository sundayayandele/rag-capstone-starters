# Incident Response Policy

## Reporting
Security incidents must be reported to the Security Operations Centre within 1 hour of detection. Personal data breaches must be notified to the data protection officer within 24 hours so that the supervisory authority can be informed within 72 hours.

## Severity
Severity 1 incidents involve customer data loss or a service outage of more than 4 hours and need an executive briefing within 2 hours.
