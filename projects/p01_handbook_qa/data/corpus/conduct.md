# Code of Conduct

## Respectful workplace
Harassment, discrimination and bullying are not tolerated. Concerns can be raised with your manager, HR, or the confidential ethics line.

## Gifts
Gifts from suppliers worth more than 50 euros must be declared to the compliance team and may not be accepted without approval.

## Conflicts of interest
Declare any outside business activity to your manager. Employees may not work for a competitor while employed.

## Whistleblowing
Reports to the ethics line can be anonymous. Retaliation against a person who reports in good faith leads to disciplinary action.
