# Expenses Policy

## Travel
Book flights and hotels through the approved travel portal. Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours. Hotel stays are capped at 150 euros per night in standard cities and 220 euros per night in high-cost cities.

## Meals
The meal allowance while travelling is 60 euros per day. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

## Claims
Submit expense claims within 30 days of the expense with receipts attached. Claims above 500 euros need approval from a department head. Reimbursement is paid with the next monthly payroll.

## Home office
A one-time home office allowance of 300 euros is available to employees on a remote or hybrid arrangement.
