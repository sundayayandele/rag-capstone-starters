# Leave Policy

## Annual leave
Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues monthly, and up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the next year. Carried-over days expire on 31 March.

## Requesting leave
Submit leave requests in the HR portal at least 10 working days before the planned start date. Your manager approves or declines within 3 working days. Requests longer than 10 consecutive days need director approval.

## Sick leave
Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required after 3 consecutive sick days. Notify your manager before 9:00 on the first day of absence.

## Parental leave
Primary carers receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave and secondary carers receive 4 weeks. Leave may start up to 4 weeks before the due date.
