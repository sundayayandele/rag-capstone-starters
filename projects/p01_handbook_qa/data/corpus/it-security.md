# IT Security

## Passwords
Passwords must be at least 14 characters. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for all accounts. Passwords are never shared.

## Devices
Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the IT service desk within 2 hours. Company laptops use full-disk encryption.

## Phishing
Report suspicious emails with the Report Phishing button. Never enter credentials on a link from an unexpected message.

## Data classification
Documents are classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Restricted data may not be stored on personal devices or in personal cloud accounts.
