# Remote and Hybrid Work

## Eligibility
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week after completing the 3-month probation period. Fully remote arrangements need written approval from the department head.

## Core hours
Everyone must be available between 10:00 and 15:00 local time on working days.

## Working from abroad
Working from another country is permitted for up to 20 days per calendar year with manager approval. Longer periods require approval from HR and Legal because of tax and social security rules.

## Equipment
The company provides a laptop and a monitor. Employees must use the company VPN when accessing internal systems.
