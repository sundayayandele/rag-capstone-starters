# Benefits

## Pension
The company contributes 6 percent of base salary to the pension plan if the employee contributes at least 3 percent. Enrolment is automatic after probation.

## Health insurance
All employees receive private health insurance. Dependants can be added for 45 euros per month each.

## Learning budget
Each employee has a learning budget of 1,200 euros per year for courses, books and conferences. Unused budget does not roll over.

## Wellbeing
The employee assistance programme offers free confidential counselling, 6 sessions per issue.
